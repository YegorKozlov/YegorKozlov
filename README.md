## Hi there 👋

I'm an AEM Architect with over 10 years of experience, and an opensource enthusiast. 
 
I’ve been working in the IT industry for more than 20 years and started my first AEM project back in 2013. Since then, AEM has been growing as a product, and I have been growing along with it, constantly learning, attending conferences, adopting new features, and contributing bugs to the open-sourced parts of AEM. 
 
Seeking an opensource Java API to generate MS Office documents, I joined the Apache POI project in 2006, and became heavily involved in the development. I have previously served as the Chairman and Release Manager of Apache POI for 4 years, and I am a member of the Apache Software Foundation since 2010.
 
I am also a committer in the Adobe ACS Commons project and presented my contributions at adaptTo() 2021 and adaptTo() 2023 conferences.
